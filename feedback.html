Feedback

This website was created to preserve and celebrate the history of St. Xavier Cross Country and distance track. It was started in 2025 by the parent of a now-graduated StX athlete, with the hope that it will continue to grow and live on for future athletes, families, and coaches.

This is a volunteer project built without formal web-development experience, so some features and capabilities may be limited. The site is still evolving, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.

Every effort has been made to gather as much historical information as possible, but some results may be incomplete or missing.

If you notice missing information, find an error, or have an idea for improving the site, please email Bob Sanders at sanderisnky@gmail.com.

If you are an StX parent, alumnus, coach, or supporter interested in helping maintain—or eventually taking over—this website, please reach out. The goal is for this resource to remain useful and continue long after its original creator is no longer actively involved.
